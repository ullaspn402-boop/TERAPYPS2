Slide 2: Clinical Problem Statement
Slide 2 of 10
Visual Layout
3-Column Comparison: High Supervisor Ratios, Fragmented Care, Language Barriers.
Presenter Script (What to say)
"In clinical education and healthcare settings today, we face three major bottlenecks:
1. High Student-to-Supervisor Ratios: Clinical supervisors are overwhelmed reviewing physical SOAP notes, treatment plans, and tracking student competencies manually.
2. Patient Disengagement & Limited At-Home Practice: Patients often lack interactive, feedback-driven practice tools between in-clinic therapy sessions.
3. Linguistic & Regional Barriers: Millions of patients in multilingual regions cannot access digital therapy tools in their native languages."
Key Talking Points
  • Manual supervision slows down student clinical evaluations.
  • Lack of objective home practice metrics delays patient recovery.
  • Language mismatch creates compliance barriers in healthcare delivery.
